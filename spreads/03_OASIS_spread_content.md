## SHEET 2 — the problem + the idea

**The story (~50 words — this is the emotional core, set it well):**
Three professionals, three datasets, zero conversations. An architect opens EnergyPlus. A public health officer opens a demographic spreadsheet. A housing operator opens a maintenance log. And an 82-year-old woman in Apartment 4B sleeps in 32° heat — invisible to all three. The data to save her exists. Just never in the same room.

**Numbers strip (big, mono):**
546,000 heat deaths/year worldwide · 50–85% linked to indoor exposure · 591,211 Barcelona homes lack cross-ventilation · 28% classified heat-vulnerable

**The idea (~30 words):**
An AI-assisted retrofit workflow that maps heat vulnerability across scales and ranks passive interventions using climate, building, and health data — one connected system from city block to bedroom.

**Three layers (diagram labels):**
Layer 1 — URBAN: where is the crisis? Zone HVI, urban interventions
Layer 2 — BUILDING: which rooms overheat? IFC diagnosis, ranked retrofits
Layer 3 — UNIT: what can a resident do? Phone-scan assessment (future)

**Users (one line):** Municipalities → retrofit agencies → residents. Each layer has its own client.

# SPREAD B — the proof

## SHEET 3 — the system

**Layer 1, urban (~40 words):**
Draw a zone. In a minute, OASIS fuses live satellite, cadastral, and census data into a 12-factor vulnerability index per building — then tests urban measures first. Trees, cool surfaces, shade. Only buildings still above the risk gate escalate to Layer 2.

**Decision gate (small callout):**
Zone HVI < 5.5 after urban measures → building retrofit not required. Triage before spending.

**Layer 2, building (~40 words):**
Upload the IFC model. OASIS reads rooms, facades, and neighbours' shadows, then scores each room on health-weighted metrics — operative temperature, WBGT, nocturnal recovery, age-weighted overheating hours. An LLM writes the plain-language diagnosis and ranks the top three passive retrofits per room.

**Output row (one line):**
Risk-coloured 3D rooms · before/after retrofit renders · ventilation schedules · costed strategy report

## SHEET 4 — beyond the tool

**Market (one line, big numbers):**
12M vulnerable EU homes · 4.6M pre-1980 units in Spain · 591k in Barcelona alone

**Positioning (~30 words):**
Existing tools either simulate buildings or map heat. OASIS is heat-specific by design and multi-scale by architecture: one connected system from urban triage to room-level prescription, priced from city licence to €16 per unit.

**The flywheel (one line):**
Every resident assessment feeds ground-truth data back to the city's model — a civic resilience loop.

**Closing quote:**
"The data to save her existed all along. OASIS puts it in the same room."

**Footer:** Barcelona is the demo, not the boundary — data loaders are swappable, the method is global.
