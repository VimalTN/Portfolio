## SHEET 1 — the idea

**Title:** THE GROUND BENEATH GROWTH
**Kicker:** AI for All · UIA Barcelona World Capital of Architecture
**Subtitle:** An interactive installation that lets the public decide the future of Catalonia's last great coastal wetland

**Meta line:**
IAAC · MaAI · 2026 — Vimal TN ⚠️ + team — Llobregat Delta, Barcelona–El Prat

**Problem (~30 words):**
In 40 years the Llobregat Delta lost nearly half its wetland — and the life that lived in it. The proposed third runway would reach into what remains. Who gets to decide?

**The installation (~40 words):**
A tablet embedded in the table edge drives a projection cast onto a laser-engraved terrain board of the airport. Visitors walk a hook → explore → decide sequence: watch 40 years of change unfold beneath their hands, then vote on the delta's 2050.

**Four lenses (labels for a diagram or strip):**
LAND — wetland sealed under runway and city, 1985–2020
HEAT — sealed surfaces bake ~20°C hotter than the wetland
TRAFFIC — flight corridors thickening over the lagoons, live aircraft overhead
LIFE — bird species fading as habitat is lost

**Stats strip:**
40 years of change · 4 data lenses · 8 live public datasets · 1 vote per visitor

## SHEET 2 — the proof

**Nothing is invented (~40 words) — the differentiator, keep it:**
Every layer is real public data: historical aerial photographs from 1956 onward, Landsat surface temperature, 30 m land cover, GBIF bird records — and live aircraft positions streaming from OpenSky as visitors watch. The base map itself ages as the years advance.

**How it runs (one line):**
One server, two synced surfaces — the tablet posts its state, the projection mirrors it instantly, and votes accumulate into a living public record.

**The decision (3 short labels):**
01 GROWTH — build the runway, accept the loss
02 COMPROMISE — limited expansion, partial restoration
03 PRESERVE — protect what's left, let it recover

**There is no neutral choice (small callout):**
Each path has a cost and a gain — the installation refuses to editorialize; the tally is the exhibit.

**Closing quote:**
"The data doesn't tell you what to choose. It makes sure you know what you're choosing."

**Footer:** Python · FastAPI · Leaflet · SSE · live APIs — no build step, runs anywhere
