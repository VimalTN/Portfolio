## SHEET 1 — the idea

**Title:** THE GROUND BENEATH GROWTH
**Kicker:** AI for All · UIA Barcelona World Capital of Architecture
**Subtitle:** Visualizing the ecological cost of growth — the case of Barcelona–El Prat and the Llobregat Delta

**Hypothesis (pull-quote):**
"What are we willing to sacrifice for growth?"

**Meta line:**
IAAC · MaAI · AI for All · 2026 — Vimal TN, Seid Burka, Sahil Yousaf, Rafik ⚠️ surname

**Problem (~35 words):**
A decision is being made about land most people never see. The Llobregat Delta — Catalonia's last great coastal wetland — lost nearly half its wetland in 40 years. A third runway would reach into what remains. The debate runs on slogans, not shared understanding.

**The installation (~40 words):**
A table you lean over; a board the light falls on. A tablet embedded in the table edge drives a projection registered to a laser-engraved terrain board of El Prat. Visitors walk a three-act arc — hook, explore, decide — then vote on the delta's 2050.

**Four lenses (strip labels):**
LAND — wetland sealed under runway and city
HEAT — sealed surfaces ~20°C hotter than the wetland
TRAFFIC — corridors thickening, live aircraft overhead
LIFE — bird species fading as habitat is lost

**Stats strip:**
221 km² study area · 9 epochs, 1985–2025 · 9 public datasets · 1 vote per visitor

## SHEET 2 — the proof

**Nothing is invented (~35 words):**
Every layer is authoritative public data — aerial photographs from 1956, Landsat land cover across four decades, GBIF bird records, aircraft streaming live from OpenSky every 30 seconds. The base map itself ages as the years advance.

**Built, not mocked up (one line):**
One FastAPI server, two synced surfaces — the tablet posts state, Server-Sent Events mirror it to the projection instantly, and the vote tally grows as a living public record.

**Installation specs (small mono block):**
Engraved board 0.80 × 0.60 m · counter-height table 1050 mm · vertical projector, 2.5 m drop · edge-embedded tablet

**Findings (3 short rows — from the deck, keep these):**
The data already tells the story — we didn't dramatise it; we revealed it
Touch beats dashboards — one clear choice engages where charts lose people
Honest, not persuasive — three futures, no verdict; visitors argue with each other, which is the point

**The decision (3 labels):**
01 GROWTH — build the runway, accept the loss
02 COMPROMISE — limited expansion, partial restoration
03 PRESERVE — protect what's left, let it recover

**Closing quote:**
"The ground beneath growth is never free. This installation lets everyone weigh the cost — together."

**Footer:** Python · FastAPI · Leaflet · SSE · TiTiler · no build step — offline-first on exhibition day
