## SHEET 1 — the idea

**Title:** SMART FARMING
**Subtitle:** An autonomous cultivation module driven by an AI brain — deployable on any available surface

**Meta line:**
IAAC · MaAI Research Studio · 2026 — Vimal TN, Juan Gaitán, Rojda Gulel

**Problem (~35 words):**
Agriculture drinks more than 70% of global freshwater, and 60% of irrigation water never reaches a plant. Cities pushed food far away — Barcelona grows 77% less than in the 1970s while its rooftops sit empty.

**Opportunity (one line, big number):**
1,764 hectares of farmable rooftop potential in Barcelona alone.

**The module (~40 words):**
A 4×4 m plug-and-play greenhouse: solar-powered, rainwater-harvesting, land-blind — rooftops, parking lots, degraded ground. Each module runs its own edge AI for life-critical functions and connects to a city-wide brain for strategy.

**Operations strip (4 step labels):**
Onboard → recipe injection → autonomous orchestration → harvest

**Stats strip:**
4×4 m module · 60% water saving target · 1,764 ha potential

## SHEET 2 — the proof

**System (~40 words):**
Three specialized models run each module: a neural network predicts water and nutrient doses, an SVM classifies disease and stress from multispectral imagery, and a random forest schedules the city-wide network. A fusion engine turns their outputs into one decision — opening valves, mixing nutrients, alerting the farmer.

**The honesty story (~35 words) — this is your differentiator, keep it:**
Our random forest first scored 99% — a leaked feature, not intelligence. We injected noise, broke the shortcut, and reported the honest number. Real agricultural data is chaotic; we chose proof over polish.

**Results (3 rows, table style):**
SVM disease detection — 89% accuracy
RF scheduling — 40.46%, K-fold ±0.0074 (honest and generalizable)
ANN regression — R² −0.06 · real farm data resists simple models

**Demo (one line):**
A digital-twin dashboard with an AI assistant scales from one rooftop to a 233-module Barcelona network.

**Closing quote:**
"A module to be plugged and played everywhere."

**Footer:** risks managed: sensor drift · algorithmic monoculture · logical quarantine
