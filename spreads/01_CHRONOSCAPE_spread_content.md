## SHEET 1 — the idea

**Title:** CHRONOSCAPE
**Subtitle:** Rebuilding reality from fragments of memory and lived experience

**Meta line:**
IAAC · MaAI Research Studio · 2025 — Vimal TN, Nithik Vairamuthu, Jinesh Narendra Jain, Seid Burka

**Hypothesis (pull-quote):**
"Incorporating memory into 3D reconstruction yields environments with greater contextual richness and spatial coherence than image-only or text-only models."

**Problem (~40 words):**
AI reconstructs lost cities by hallucinating — filling gaps with generic street patterns and idealized styles from its training data. The result looks nothing like the place that was lost. Memory grounds the AI in the lived city, not a statistical average.

**Case study (one line):**
Testbed: Pacific Palisades, 2025 — 23,000 acres and thousands of homes lost to wildfire.

**Workflow (5 step labels, for the pipeline strip):**
Memory intake → prompt parser → location enhancer → architectural references → collective memory layer

**Stats strip:**
250 context images · 82 narratives · 13 architectural elements ⚠️ confirm labels
