## NOTES ON THE "LANGUAGE" WE'LL USE

Every project will follow the same voice so the portfolio reads as one
coherent body of work:

1. **Hook first** — one sentence on what it does / why it matters (not
   "this project was done in Term 1...").
2. **Problem → approach → outcome** — 2–3 sentences max on the card;
   depth lives in the spreads/images.
3. **Active, concrete verbs** — "detects", "generates", "evaluates" —
   not "aims to explore".
4. **Tags carry the tech** — so descriptions stay readable, not a
   keyword dump.
