OASIS

Overheating Assessment System for Intervention Strategies

One connected system for heat adaptation — from city triage to a single room.

Draw a zone and OASIS fuses live satellite, cadastral, and census data into a building-level heat-vulnerability index. It tests urban measures first — trees, cool surfaces, shade — and a decision gate rules on whether building retrofit is even needed. Buildings that stay at risk escalate to room-by-room IFC diagnosis and ranked passive retrofits.

The Ground Beneath Growth

Visualizing the ecological cost of growth — Barcelona–El Prat & the Llobregat Delta

An interactive installation that lets the public decide the future of Catalonia's last great coastal wetland.

Forty years of real geospatial data — land, heat, traffic, life — projected onto a laser-engraved terrain board, driven by a tablet embedded in the table edge. Visitors walk a three-act arc: observe, explore, decide. Every layer is authoritative public data, with aircraft streaming live from OpenSky as they watch. Then they vote on the delta's 2050.

Plug & Grow

An autonomous cultivation module driven by an AI brain

A plug-and-play cultivation module that turns any idle surface into farmland.

A 4×4 m solar-powered greenhouse for rooftops and leftover urban ground. Three ML models — water prediction, disease detection, and network scheduling — fuse into autonomous decisions, scaling from a single unit to a 233-module Barcelona grid. Tested against real, honestly-reported agricultural data: we caught our own 99% as data leakage and published the honest 40%.

2025 Research Studio

Chronoscape

Rebuilding reality from fragments of memory and lived experience

An image-generation workflow that treats memory as design input for urban regeneration.

Tested on the 2025 Palisades Fire, Chronoscape structures residents' narratives and photographs into a memory layer that grounds AI reconstruction in the lived city — preserving real street geometry and neighbourhood identity where standard models hallucinate generic form. It scales from a single resident's home to collective, neighbourhood-scale memoryscapes.

2026 Hardware II MRAC / MaAI

RE:PAIR

Perception-driven, decision-visible closed-loop robotic repair

A robot that sees a crack, plans the fix, repairs it, and checks its own work.

A YOLOv8 model detects and masks structural cracks; a UR10 arm 3D-prints the repair and re-scans to verify. Every decision is surfaced live in a ROS2 observability interface, so the robot stops behaving like a black box. The hard part wasn't the model — it was integration: a discipline, not a bug.
